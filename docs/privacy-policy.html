Privacy Policy — Video Finder & Playlists

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Video Finder & Playlists ("the app") is developed by Bencherk Aboubakeur ("we"). This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

1. Data stored only on your device

Videos you save to your library (title and link) are stored only in your phone's internal storage. They never leave your device and we cannot access them.

2. Data collected by third-party services

  • Google AdMob: displays ads and may collect the advertising ID, approximate location (derived from IP) and device information to serve ads. Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics: collects anonymous usage statistics and crash reports to improve stability.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: stores a device token to deliver optional push notifications.

3. Consent (EEA / UK users)

Before ads are personalized, the app shows a Google consent message (User Messaging Platform). You may choose personalized ads, non-personalized ads, or decline. Your choice is stored by Google and can be reset by clearing the app data.

4. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

5. Data deletion

To request deletion of analytics data associated with your device, email: YOUR_EMAIL@example.com. On-device library data is deleted at any time by uninstalling the app.

6. Security

All network traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). We do not sell personal data.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
