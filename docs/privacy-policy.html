Privacy Policy — Video Finder

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Video Finder ("the app") is developed by Bencherk Aboubakeur ("we"). The app lets you save video links, organise them with tags and notes, and open them in the official YouTube app or your web browser. The app does not play, download or host any video.

1. Data stored only on your device

Your saved links, titles, tags, notes and watched status are stored only in your phone's internal app storage. They are never uploaded to us and we cannot access them. You can export or delete them at any time inside the app, or by uninstalling it.

2. Data collected by third-party services

  • Google AdMob — displays ads and may collect the advertising ID, approximate location derived from IP address, and device information. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Analytics — collects app interaction and device information for usage statistics.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — collects crash stack traces and device state to improve stability.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — stores a device token to deliver optional push notifications.

3. Consent (EEA / UK)

Before ads are personalised, the app shows a Google consent message (User Messaging Platform). You may choose personalised ads, non-personalised ads, or decline.

4. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

5. Data deletion

To request deletion of analytics or crash data associated with your device, email Toukiworld@gmail.com. On-device library data is removed by deleting items in the app or uninstalling it.

6. Security

All network traffic uses HTTPS; cleartext HTTP is disabled. We do not sell personal data.

7. Third-party content

Links you save point to YouTube. Opening a link launches the official YouTube application or your browser, which are governed by Google's own terms and privacy policy. Video Finder is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by YouTube or Google.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
